Privacy Policy — Hot Sauce Collection

Effective date: August 31, 2026
Operated by: Rumaner Consulting, LLC
Contact: rrumaner@gmail.com

Overview

Hot Sauce Collection is a personal hot-sauce collection manager. The app works fully offline, requires no account, and does not sync your collection to any server or other device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account, sign-up, name, email address, or phone number is required or collected.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No cloud sync — nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following stays on your device only and is never uploaded:

  • Sauces and brands you add, including heat levels
  • Favorites and wish-list items
  • Tasting notes and tags
  • Heat-level changes and hidden or moved sauces
  • Bottle photos you choose to add

Photos

If you add a bottle photo, the photo is chosen with your device's system photo picker and stored locally on your device. Photos are never uploaded or shared.

Exports

Excel exports of your collection are generated locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Hot Sauce Collection is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published here with a revised effective date.
